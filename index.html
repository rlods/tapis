Tapis

Real rugs and curtains for your Mac desktop.

Tapis lays a soft, cloth-simulated rug on your desktop, above your icons and below every window. Grab a corner and fold it. Curl it up. Sweep things under it. Or hang curtains from the menu bar, and draw them across your desktop.

Download for Mac Free · macOS 14 or later · Apple silicon

Now you see it. Now you don’t.

It behaves like fabric

Fold and curl

Grab any part and drag to lift, fold or curl it. Drag a fold back to undo it, or double-click to smooth it all out.

Drapes over your icons

Each desktop icon makes a bump under the rug. Piles of icons make bigger bumps.

Design your own

Persian, Heriz, Kilim, Berber, Stripes, Bordered, Beach Towel, Doormat or Shag, with your choice of colours, fringe, wear and pile length.

Stack several rugs

Rugs rest on one another, and the one you move slides on top.

Curtains too

Hang curtains from the menu bar. Slide them along it, draw them open or closed, swish them: they hang back in their folds. Velvet, linen, stripes, bordered or your own picture.

Several arrangements

Keep a set of rugs or curtains for each mood, one for work and one for the weekend, and switch with a click in the menu bar.

🔈Fabric sounds. A soft, muffled wool swish while it moves, and a gentle thump when a fold lands. One click to turn off.

🍃Light on your Mac. A rug at rest uses next to no CPU. No fans, no battery drain, even though it lives on your desktop all day.

How to use it

Everything happens with the mouse on the rug.

  1. Fold it

    Grab any part of the rug and drag it to lift, fold or curl it. Folds stay where you leave them.

    Drag a fold back to undo it. Double-click to smooth the whole rug out.

  2. Long press to edit

    Press and hold on a rug for half a second: it’s selected, and won’t fold while you edit it.

    Drag to move it, drag a corner to resize, drag the top knob to rotate (hold ⇧ to snap by 15°). Click it again, or anywhere else, when you’re done.

  3. Make it yours

    A selected rug has a small toolbar underneath. Design opens the pattern, colours, fringe, wear and pile length.

    The toolbar also turns it 90°, smooths it out, or removes it.

  4. Slide it under your icons

    By default a rug lies on top of your desktop icons, which make bumps under it. It can also lie below them, like a mat under your files.

    In the toolbar, choose Place below icons. To reach it again, pick Rearrange Rugs in the menu.

  5. Add more rugs

    Choose Add Rug in the menu (⌘N). Rugs pile up on one another, and the one you move slides on top.

    Untick Show Rugs to clear the desktop for a moment.

  6. Hang curtains

    Choose Curtains in the menu: a curtain hangs from the menu bar. Drag it sideways to slide it along, and into another to push it.

    ⌘-double-click draws it open or closed, double-click smooths it. Press and hold it to resize it by its side handles. Add Curtain hangs another.

  7. Switch arrangements

    An arrangement is a set of rugs, or of curtains. Keep one for work and one for the weekend, and switch with a click in the menu.

    Manage Arrangements… creates, duplicates, renames and deletes them. Everything saves on its own.

Install

Free. Requires macOS 14 or later on Apple silicon.

  1. Download

    Get the latest DMG from the Releases page.

  2. Drag to Applications

    Open the DMG and drag Tapis into your Applications folder.

  3. Launch it

    Open Tapis from Applications. Its icon appears in the menu bar.

  4. Stay up to date

    Tapis tells you when a new version is out and offers to update.

Privacy by design

No account. No analytics. The only network call is the update check, and it sends nothing about you. Updating is always your choice. Tapis runs entirely on your Mac, and your rugs and curtains never leave it.

🏠

Saved on your Mac

Your rugs, curtains, designs and arrangements are kept on your Mac only. Nothing is synced or uploaded.

🗂

Your desktop stays yours

Tapis asks Finder where your icons are, only so the rug can drape over them. That never leaves your Mac.

🔄

Just one check, for updates

To see if there’s a new version, Tapis reads a public list on GitHub. It sends nothing about you or your Mac.
